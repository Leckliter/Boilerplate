## One person, one contact

Everyone we deal with — customers, leads, staff — is one **contact**. What someone _is_ is described with **tags**, such as customer or lead. Tags only describe: they never open any doors.

## Access comes from roles

What a person may do in Admin depends on their **role**, not their tags. A person with no role is a customer: they can use their own account and nothing else.

## Messages

When something happens that you should hear about, the app records a notification in your inbox and may also send an email, text or push message, depending on your settings. Texts need your recorded agreement. Messages sent late at night wait until the morning.

## Everything is an action

Each thing the app can do has a name, like `contacts.create`. The website, the API and AI assistants all use the same actions, so the same rules apply everywhere.

## About these docs

Docs are short Markdown files. Each says who it is for: **public** docs are for everyone and **internal** docs are for the team. Internal docs are hidden from everyone else, including in search and over the API.
