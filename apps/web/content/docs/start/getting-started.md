Welcome. This page shows you around. Replace it with your own introduction when you make the app yours.

## Signing in

Go to **Sign in** and enter your email address. We email you a short code: type it in and you are signed in. There is no password to remember.

## Where things are

- **Your account** (`/account`) is for everyone with a login: your details and your notifications.
- **Admin** (`/admin`) is for the team. You only see it if you have been given a role.
- **Docs** (`/docs`) is where you are now.

## Notifications

We only message you about things that matter. Open **Account → Notifications** to choose email, text or push for each kind of message. Texts are only sent if you have said yes, and you can reply **STOP** to any text to turn them off.

## Need help?

Read the [handbook](/docs/handbook/how-this-app-works), or contact the team that gave you this app.
